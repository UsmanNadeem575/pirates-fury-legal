Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is, and is not, collected in connection with your download and use of Pirates Fury, our mobile application, including its optional in-app purchase and optional rewarded advertisements.

Application: Pirates Fury

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Publisher: DevMasterMind

Table of Contents

    1. Overview

    This Privacy Policy describes how DevMasterMind, operated by Muhammad Usman ("Developer," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information in connection with Pirates Fury (the "Application").

    The Application is free to download. The first stage is free to play. Later stages can be unlocked in one of two ways:

    • PRO (in-app purchase): a one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks all stages permanently and removes all advertisements.
    • Rewarded advertisement: watching an optional video advertisement, served by Google AdMob, to unlock a single stage for the current play session.

    The Application does not require you to create an account, and the Developer does not operate any server that collects your personal information. Information used for purchases and advertising is collected and processed by Google, as described below.

    2. Information the Developer Does Not Collect

    The Developer does not collect, store, or receive any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

    The Application does not include analytics tracking or social media integrations. The only third-party services in the Application are Google Play (including Google Play Billing for the in-app purchase) and Google AdMob (for optional rewarded advertisements), described in Sections 4 and 5.

    3. Locally Stored Game Data

    To save your progress, the Application stores certain data on your device using Android's standard local storage mechanism (PlayerPrefs), such as:

    • your currently unlocked stage and in-game settings; and
    • a protected (hashed) record of whether you own the PRO purchase, so that a paying player can continue to play offline.

    This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to the Developer or any third party, and it is permanently and automatically removed if you uninstall the Application. Stages unlocked by watching an advertisement are not saved; they are kept only until the Application is closed.

    4. In-App Purchase and Google Play

    The Application is distributed through the Google Play Store and offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase ("PRO"). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. When you download the Application or make a purchase, Google Play independently collects certain information, such as your purchase history, device information, and payment details, in order to process your transaction and deliver the purchase to you.

    The Application asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the PRO purchase and checks Google's digital signature on the purchase receipt on your device. The Developer does not receive or store your payment card, billing details, or Google account information at any point.

    This information is collected and processed directly by Google, in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

    5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

    Players who do not own PRO may choose to watch a rewarded video advertisement to unlock a stage. Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Advertisements are only requested when a locked stage is reached. Players who own PRO never see advertisements, and the advertising service is not started for them.

    When advertisements are requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google the following information:

    • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
    • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
    • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version;
    • Ad interaction data, such as ad views, taps, and whether a video was watched to completion; and
    • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data related to the advertising service.

    Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertisements, to personalize advertisements where you have allowed it, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve its services. This information is sent to Google, not to the Developer, and is encrypted in transit. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

    Watching an advertisement is always optional. An advertisement unlocks a stage only if it is watched to the end, and advertisement availability is not guaranteed.

    6. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

    If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized advertisements are shown. You may accept or decline. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized advertisements, which use limited data for purposes such as fraud prevention and frequency capping.

    Your consent choice is stored on your device and can be reset by clearing the Application's data or reinstalling the Application, after which you will be asked again.

    7. Device Permissions

    The Application does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or call and SMS logs.

    The Application uses the following standard permissions: internet and network-state access (to check purchases with Google Play and to load advertisements), Google Play Billing (for the in-app purchase), and the Advertising ID permission (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Section 5).

    8. Children's Privacy

    The Application is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    Parents and guardians should refer to the Application's official content rating on the Google Play Store before allowing a minor to download or use it. If you believe a child has provided information through the Application, please contact us using the details in Section 14.

    9. Data Security

    Because the Developer does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side database of user information for the Developer to maintain or secure. Information handled by Google Play and Google AdMob is protected under Google's own security practices.

    10. Third-Party Services

    The Application uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

    • Google Play Store and Google Play Billing — distribution and in-app purchase. Google Privacy Policy
    • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform — optional rewarded advertisements and consent. How Google uses information from partner apps

    If additional third-party services are added in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be revised accordingly before that update is released.

    11. Your Choices

    • Avoid advertisements: watching advertisements is optional. Purchasing PRO removes all advertisements.
    • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized advertising, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
    • Google ad settings: you can manage how Google personalizes advertisements at adssettings.google.com.
    • Local data: you may remove all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the Application. Your PRO purchase remains linked to your Google account and can be restored with the "Restore Purchase" option.

    Because the Developer does not hold an account or personal data about you, there is no personal data held by the Developer for you to access, correct, or delete. Requests regarding data collected by Google should be made to Google through its own tools.

    12. International Users

    The Application is offered globally through the Google Play Store. The Developer does not collect or process your personal data, so no cross-border transfer of personal data by the Developer occurs. Google may process information collected through Google Play and Google AdMob in countries other than your own, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

    13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Application or applicable legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

    Your continued use of the Application after any such changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
